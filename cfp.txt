CALL FOR PAPERS

ML4FS: Machine Learning with and for Fuzzy Sets
Special session at IFSA-EUSFLAT 2027
Joint 22nd IFSA World Congress and 15th EUSFLAT Conference
Bari, Italy, 13-17 September 2027

https://ml4fs.github.io

Paper submission deadline: 14 February 2027 (AoE)


SCOPE

Machine learning and fuzzy set theory offer complementary perspectives on
learning from complex, uncertain, and gradually structured data. Fuzzy sets
give machine-learning systems principled ways to represent vagueness, graded
concepts, uncertain labels, and linguistic knowledge. Conversely,
machine-learning techniques make it possible to construct, adapt, validate,
and compress fuzzy sets and fuzzy systems directly from data.

ML4FS brings together researchers working at this two-way interface. We
welcome contributions on methods in which fuzzy representations improve
learning, robustness, interpretability, and human-machine interaction. We
equally welcome methods that use statistical, kernel-based, evolutionary,
neural, and other machine-learning techniques to induce membership functions,
fuzzy rules, aggregation operators, and granular representations.

Particular attention will be devoted to the methodological foundations of
data-driven fuzzy-set construction: the semantics and identifiability of
learned membership degrees, the incorporation of expert knowledge and
structural constraints, the evaluation and comparison of induced fuzzy sets,
and the trade-off between accuracy, interpretability, complexity, and
computational efficiency. Applications are also welcome, for example in
trustworthy AI, knowledge representation, bioinformatics, anomaly detection,
natural-language processing, decision support, and resource-constrained
intelligent systems.


TOPICS

Topics of interest include, but are not limited to:

- Data-driven induction of fuzzy sets and membership functions
- Machine learning from fuzzy, imprecise, or partially reliable data
- Learning with graded, ambiguous, weak, or uncertain labels
- Fuzzy classification, regression, clustering, and feature learning
- Neuro-fuzzy and deep fuzzy systems
- Evolutionary and optimization-based learning of fuzzy systems
- Learning fuzzy rules, linguistic models, and aggregation operators
- Granular computing and shadowed-set learning
- Interpretability and explainability of fuzzy machine-learning models
- Statistical validation and evaluation of learned fuzzy sets
- Robustness, reliability, calibration, and uncertainty quantification
- Complexity reduction and compression of fuzzy models
- Online, incremental, and evolving fuzzy learning
- Hybrid knowledge-driven and data-driven learning
- Applications of fuzzy machine learning in scientific and societal domains
